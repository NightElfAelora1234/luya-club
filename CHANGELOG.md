# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

Initial open-source release.

- Build-free three.js r186 South China Sea dive + first-person lure fishing.
- Five concept rods, five procedural lures, twelve species guide with conservation rules.
- 6 / 8 / 12-round size-based QTE, Space-only hook-set, 200 m spool, click-in-circle netting.
- Line-run lock for strong pelagics, rod/leader gear matching and cut-line escapes.
- Clickable descent campaign plaques and a lit, smoothly drifting seabed sign.
- Procedural WebAudio SFX and a 90-second seamlessly looping sea theme.
- MIT license, GitHub Pages workflow and low-end quality preset.
